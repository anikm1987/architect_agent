Atlas Insurance (£680M GWP, UK GI, Manchester) is under PRA enhanced supervision following a near-miss on a Q3 Solvency II return (data reconciliation failure). The firm is undertaking a major digital transformation: replacing COBOL/iSeries core policy admin with Guidewire Cloud Suite and adding a cloud-native API/digital engagement layer.

**Key regulatory facts established as of 2026-04-29:**

- SS1/21 compliance deadline passed 31 March 2025 — firms must now *demonstrate* they remain within impact tolerances (not transitional). No formal amendment to SS1/21 framework itself.
- PS7/26 (PRA) / PS26/2 (FCA) published 18 March 2026 — new operational incident reporting and material third-party (MTP) notification/register regime, effective 18 March 2027. Guidewire Cloud will constitute an MTP arrangement requiring pre-contract notification via FCA Connect.
- SS2/21 updated March 2026 (effective March 2027) — outsourcing/third-party framework strengthened; Chapter 7 specifically addresses cloud. SaaS platforms like Guidewire are in scope as material non-outsourcing third-party arrangements.
- CTP regime (PS16/24 / PS24/16) live from January 2025 but HM Treasury has not yet designated any CTPs. No cloud provider or insurance SaaS platform is currently designated. PRA Business Plan 2026/27 commits to advancing first designations.
- Enhanced supervision status likely places Atlas in the "enhanced tier" for PS7/26 incident reporting (three-phase reporting vs single report for standard firms).
- PRA/FCA joint ICT/cyber risk consultation was planned for H2 2025 — status unconfirmed as of April 2026. Open gap.
- DyGIST (dynamic GI stress test) runs May 2026 — overlaps with Atlas Phase 1.
- Solvent Exit Analysis (SEA) required by 30 June 2026 for in-scope insurers — parallel compliance obligation.

**Why:** Establishes the regulatory baseline for all architecture recommendations.
**How to apply:** Ground all resilience, third-party, and cloud architecture recommendations in these facts. Check for PS7/26 pre-contract notification timing whenever Guidewire procurement milestones are discussed.
